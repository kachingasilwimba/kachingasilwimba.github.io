Current work

I use Earth observation and machine learning to study how severe weather affects the power grid.

NASA-funded research · November 2026–present

Postdoctoral Research Associate

Atmospheric Sciences Research Center
University at Albany, SUNY

I’m evaluating Prithvi-EO with 30-meter Harmonized Landsat–Sentinel-2 imagery and aligning satellite, weather, and power infrastructure data. The research aims to predict where weather-related outages may occur, how severe they could be, and how long restoration may take.

About the NASA-funded project

Geospatial machine learning

TerraTorch Learning Lab

I’m building a learning lab around IBM–NASA’s Prithvi-EO foundation model. The Python workflow converts multispectral satellite scenes into patch embeddings. The browser explorer puts embedding distances alongside visible imagery and a vegetation index so you can investigate how the same location changes across dates.
